How to drive

WGo
ALeft
SBrake
DRight
  • The faster you go, the wider you turn. Brake for the sharp bends.
  • Hit a gem and you spin out. Mash W to steady the cart, or it grinds to a stop.
  • Fall in a hole and mash W to climb back out.
  • Jagged rocks crack your cart, slowing you permanently.
  • Mud and water slow you down. Mud is worse.

Tip

Breaking her ore cores

  • Only something you shoot or throw breaks an ore core.
  • Iron, emerald and diamond arrows break any ore core in one shot.
  • Weaker arrows break most in one, but the emerald and diamond ore cores take two.
  • Snowballs take seven hits each, and can't break the emerald or diamond ore cores at all.

Table talk

Click or Space

The Lava Cavern | House rules

No Limit Hold'em

  • Be the last one standing. Everyone starts with 100 big blinds.
  • The chips are ores: Iron $100 Gold $500 Ruby $1,000 Emerald $2,000 Diamond $10,000
  • Blinds start at $100 / $200 and go up every 5 minutes as the lava rises (the timer's by the buttons, top left). Under the gun you can straddle for twice the big blind, and then you act last before the flop.
  • Two cards each, then five shared cards: the flop (three), the turn and the river, with a round of betting before the flop and after each.
  • On your turn: fold, check, call, bet or raise. A raise has to be at least as big as the last bet or raise. You can bet everything you have at any time.
  • Going all in for less than a full raise doesn't reopen the betting. Side pots go to the best hand among the players in them.
  • Best five cards win: Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, Pair, High Card.
  • Seven deuce: win a pot holding seven two off suit and everyone else pays you $1,000, even if they folded.
  • After a hand nobody saw your cards in, you can show them (S). They'll remember what you show.
